About the Church

The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is centered on faith in Jesus Christ as the Son of God and Savior of the world. Members strive to follow His teachings through love, service, family unity, and devotion to God.

Missionary Work

Missionaries serve around the world, teaching about Jesus Christ, serving communities, and sharing messages of hope, faith, and charity.

Church Life & Activities

Church members participate in worship services, youth programs, family activities, humanitarian service, and community outreach.
